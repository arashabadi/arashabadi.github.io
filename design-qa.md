# Design QA

## Reference

- Selected exploded spatial-tissue hero concept supplied by the user.
- Direction: minimal, dark, three-dimensional, and progressively more detailed while scrolling.

## Verified Views

- Desktop: 1440 x 1024
- Mobile: 390 x 844
- Homepage hero, menu, About, What I do, Projects, Publications, and Connect
- Mobile project archive
- Dark default and user-selected light theme across Home and Connect

## Results

- The hero preserves the selected full-bleed composition and communicates name, alternate name, umbrella theme, and core fields in the first viewport.
- The page progresses from identity to biography, practice, projects, publications, and contact without card-heavy section styling.
- Internal homepage navigation remains in the same tab and closes the mobile menu after selection.
- The detailed publications link intentionally opens in a new tab.
- Dark mode is the site-wide default; the icon control switches themes and preserves the visitor's choice between pages.
- Text wraps without overlap and no horizontal overflow is present at either tested viewport.
- No P0, P1, or P2 issues remain.

final result: passed
